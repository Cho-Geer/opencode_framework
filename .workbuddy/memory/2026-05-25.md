## Key Finding

Previous CJK Unicode scans are unreliable for detecting Chinese language content. Box-drawing characters (─, ═), arrows (→, ←), and emoji characters within the CJK Unicode range produce thousands of false positives. Use word-level detection (Chinese common words like 的, 是, 在) for accurate results.
